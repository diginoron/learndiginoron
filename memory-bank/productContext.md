# Product Context — DigiNoron

## Why it exists
DigiNoron provides AI education (kids, teens, corporate teams) and enterprise AI implementation projects in Iran. The website is its main marketing/lead-gen channel and knowledge hub.

## Problems it solves
- Persian-language scarcity of trustworthy, deep AI analysis → solved by the bilingual AI magazine (blog).
- Lack of practical AI skills → courses pages + CTA funnels.
- Enterprise fear of AI adoption → articles de-risk adoption and drive consultation requests.

## UX Goals
- Fast, clean reading experience (Tailwind-styled HTML content, responsive tables/cards).
- Clear breadcrumbs, related articles, author credibility box (E-E-A-T).
- Persian pages RTL, English/Arabic pages correctly directed.
- Every article routes readers to `/contact` via 3 styled CTAs.
